## Layout facts
- `CharacterB2D : LevelItem (0x00, own fields end 0x94, sizeof 0x98), EmitterDelegate (vptr @0x98)`.
- Own fields 0xa0..0x510; sizeof = 0x510 (BusinessGuy/WheelchairGuy/EffectiveShopper/PogostickGuy are
  exactly 0x510; IrresponsibleDad/MopedCouple 0x518).
- EmitterDelegate secondary vtable has exactly one slot: `emitterComplete(Emitter*)` (no virtual dtor).
  Vtable word 54 (`sub_...fff68`) is the secondary table's offset-to-top (-0x98), not a function.
- No out-of-line CharacterB2D ctor: it is inline (header). Its exact member initializers are visible in
  `BusinessGuy::BusinessGuy` (@00588e74) and in the bare `new CharacterB2D` inside
  `LevelB2D::createCharacter` (@005cf084, label LAB_005cf3dc). Members NOT initialized by the ctor:
  0x2a8, 0x3d0..0x3df, 0x3e0..0x437 (emitters; zeroed in init), 0x440/0x441, 0x504.
  (operator new(0x510) without memset in createCharacter — so uninitialized members are garbage until init.)

## Creation (LevelB2D::createCharacter, M4)

bool showGore(= !UserDefault "gore_disabled"), bool flag441)`.
- characterName e.g. "wheelchair_guy", "business_guy"; vehicleName e.g. "wheelchair",
