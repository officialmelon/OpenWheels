# Level review: Lawn and Order, Idol Hands, Lift Off

Three restored-character campaign levels rebuilt to the detail level of the original campaign,
generated by `tools/levels/make_restored_levels.py` (`lm_01`, `ex_03`, `he_01`) with the new
scenery kit `tools/levels/restored_scenery.py`, and played in the game. Every screenshot below
is a frame rendered by the OpenWheels build of `main` with these levels merged (Linux, 1600 x 900),
not a mock-up.
The original-level references and the measured facts behind the designs are in
[`references/REFERENCE_NOTES.md`](references/REFERENCE_NOTES.md).

| Level | Character | Length | Shapes / specials / groups / joints / triggers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lawn and Order | Lawnmower Man (6) | 16 000 px (~18 screens) | 1925 / 19 / 1 / 1 / 10 |
| Idol Hands | Explorer Guy (7), mine cart | 16 000 px (~18 screens) | 4148 / 10 / 4 / 4 / 16 |
| Lift Off | Helicopter Man (11) | 9 400 px (~11 screens) | 1103 / 6 / 6 / 4 / 10 |

No level shows titles, hints, captions or speech bubbles. The only words are signs painted on
objects (the mailbox, NO MOWERS, COUNTY FAIR WINNER, LAWN OF THE MONTH, MAPLE COURT H.O.A.,
BAY 1 / BAY 2). The game's own tutorial arrows (system triggers from `main`) point at the control
to use at the start, before Lemonade Hill and the kiddie pool, on the temple causeway and the spike
pit rail, and at the magnet pickup and Bay 1. On PC they label the bound key in the HUD.

## Lawn and Order

Saturday on Maple Court: out of the garage, across the neighbours' sunbathing lawn and picnic,
over Lemonade Hill, through an inflatable kiddie pool (its rims catch the deck unless you lift it
with space), across the koi pond on a seesaw, through Mrs. Henderson's prize garden, past the
vegetable patch and shed, over the hedge and down to the HOA office. The inspection committee
waits just past the finish line.

## Idol Hands

The Temple of the Grasping Hand. A jungle camp and a causeway up to a carved face whose mouth is
the door. In the idol chamber a great stone arm reaches down from the roof with the golden idol
hanging from its fist. Only the idol trips the trap (b=4 triggers on the idol). When it swings,
the slab door grinds open and the frieze sheds chunks. At the top of the long descent a boulder
sits overhead on a chained trapdoor; ride under it and it drops behind you and chases you down to
a low arch it cannot pass. Then the dart corridor (three aiming dart guns), the crystal hall
(glass panes, a spike pit crossed on a rail, crystal spikes in the roof), two swinging axes, a
gallery that caves in behind you, and a breach in the outer wall into the sunset and the jeep.

## Lift Off

The Skyhook depot. Hangar 1: lift a crate with the magnet (space; shift / ctrl reel the rope) and
set it down in Bay 1. Only a crate in the bay opens the hangar door (b=4 triggers on both crates).
Hangar 2: take the heavier engine crate through the overhead line (a gantry beam, two
swinging hooks, ducting and a floor fan's updraft) up to Bay 2 on the mezzanine, which opens the
main door. Then fly out over the apron past a trainer with its engine running, under the power
lines, and land on the tower helipad. A spare crate waits in each hangar.

## Test report

All runs used the game's deterministic `--dump-world` player (`--script` control bytes,
`--dump-at` world dumps), on the build of `main` with these levels merged. The generated XML is
converted with the game's own converter first and marked offline, as the game does for campaign
levels (`Restored.cpp`), so it runs without the browser physics profile.
Two test-only additions were used locally and are **not** in the game code. They are kept as
[`test-harness.patch`](test-harness.patch):

* `OW_AUTOBALANCE`: leans the mine cart like a player keeping it level. The cart needs active
  leaning; with throttle alone it wheelies on every slope.
* `OW_UNSTICK`: plays like someone stuck on a heap: no progress for 1.5 s, so back off and drive
  on with the deck lifted.
* `OW_AUTOPILOT`: a waypoint autopilot for the helicopter. `OW_SHOTS` takes frame-exact
  screenshots.

Interactive checks used `--play-level` under Xvfb with xdotool.

### Confirmed in the game

**Lawn and Order** (merged build)
- [x] Finishes from a fresh start for 6 of 8 throttle rhythms with `OW_UNSTICK` (VICTORY 76.37 s on screen). Open-loop scripts that never back off stall on gore heaps, as a player who never reacts would.
- [x] The blade grinds the sunbathers. Whole fruit, the boombox, the picnic table and chair, the produce and the fence planks were shoved along as heaps that stalled or broke the mower, so they are art now.
- [x] The kiddie pool rims (14 px) catch the deck unless it is lifted with space.
- [x] The seesaw rests on its boarding ramp and only tips forward, so the mower rolls off onto the far bank. The old free plank flipped back up in front of the mower on the new physics.
- [x] Mrs. Henderson: knocking the pumpkin makes her throw up her arms and collapse, and the pumpkin is tossed back over the mower instead of jamming the deck.
- [x] The finale bank, the street, the finish and the committee.

**Idol Hands** (merged build, with autobalance)
- [x] Finishes from a fresh start for 5 of 10 throttle rhythms (VICTORY 15.20 s on screen). In four of the others the cart frame breaks on a hard landing (top of the descent, dart corridor, arch), and one stalls in the gallery.
- [x] Only the idol fires the trap; the slab door rises 360 px; the frieze chunks fall.
- [x] The boulder drops from its trapdoor when you pass under it, rolls down the descent and wedges at the low arch.
- [x] Darts fire and hit; glass shatters; the rail carries the cart over the spike pit; the axes swing; the gallery blocks fall behind the rider; the breach and the run-out stop the cart.
- [x] Restart (pause menu > Reset Level) returns to the camp with the door shut and the boulder back (checked on the pre-merge build).

**Lift Off**
- [x] The magnet picks up crate 1 and the copter carries it to Bay 1, and the door opens into Hangar 2 (on screen, shots 02-06, merged build).
- [x] Crate set down in Bay 1 → lamp green, door 1 rises 680 px. Engine set in Bay 2 → door 2 rises 940 px. Both doors open upwards, and the solid roofs make them real gates.
- [x] The magnet lifts the engine. The hover thrust covers cargo of several kg; the engine is 1.56 kg and the crate 0.82 kg.
- [x] Landing on the helipad: VICTORY.
- [x] Restart (pause menu > Reset Level) puts the copter back on the start pad with the timer reset (pre-merge build).

### Not confirmed

- **Lift Off as one continuous flight from start to finish.** I could not fly it automatically. The waypoint autopilot oscillates with a swinging 3 m load, and open-loop scripts drift. Each part was checked from a nearby start instead. Not checked in the game: carrying the engine under the gantry, past the hooks and duct and up onto the mezzanine (the clearances were worked out from the geometry), the fan's updraft with a load, and the power lines breaking the blades.
- The R key did not restart the level when sent with xdotool. The pause-menu Reset Level button did.
- No frame-rate measurement on low-end hardware (see the Idol Hands shape count).

### Fixes these tests drove (beyond the levels)

- `src/restored/LawnMower.cpp`: the mower hull used collision group -2 while its rider used the
  vehicle's group, so a bump smashed his chest on his own mower. The hull now shares the rider's
  group while he is on it (commit 51941b5).
- `.gitattributes`: engine patches are byte-exact on Windows checkouts. Fixes the failing
  Windows release job (commit 6035bda).
- `restored_scenery.solid_profile`: neighbouring quads overlap by 4 px (wheels caught abutting
  corners), and collinear segments become one quad (a sliding box snagged on the seams).

## Shortcomings

- **Idol Hands is heavy:** 4148 shapes, about 2.5x the largest original. Most of them are per-brick masonry; merging the bricks into fewer shapes is the obvious next step.
- **Idol Hands needs a player who leans.** The mine cart pitches up on climbs under full throttle (the scripted runs use autobalance for that reason). Nothing in the level says so, since there is no text.
- **The boulder is a threat only if you hesitate.** At full speed the cart outruns it and it is only seen overhead.
- **Lift Off's middle section is unproven**, see above. The 3 m rope swings a lot. The racks are background art the copter passes in front of.
- **Lawn and Order is chaotic.** Gore heaps in front of the deck differ from run to run; you sometimes have to back off and lift the deck to get past one.
- **Idol Hands is harder on `main`'s physics.** Half the scripted rhythms break the cart on a landing. The cart's frame-smash limit is the original's, so the descent and the gallery crest would need gentler transitions to make it forgiving.
- **The deck lift** is only hinted at by the tutorial arrow and the SLOW sign before the pool.
